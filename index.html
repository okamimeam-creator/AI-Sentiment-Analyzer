03 / THE IDEA

How it works

This mini AI model uses a transparent approach, so you can see exactly how it makes a prediction.

01

Read the text

Your sentence is split into individual words and converted to lowercase.

02

Find signals

Each word is compared with a list of words that express positive or negative feelings.

03

Compare scores

The larger score wins. If both scores match, the sentence is classified as neutral.
